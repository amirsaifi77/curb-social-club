# Gaps and Open Questions

Consolidated from the market research, architecture, product, mobile, and brand workstreams on 2026-09-05, updated the same day for the rename to Curb Social Club (ADR 0009), and updated 2026-09-06 for the scope decisions in ADR 0010 and 0011 (items 4, 5, 10, 11, 14, 23 amended; items 35 to 39 added). Items are grouped by the decision they block. Each has a suggested default so work can continue if no decision is made; the default is what the current docs assume. Resolved items stay in the table with their decision so the numbering is stable.

## Product vision

| # | Question | Why it matters | Suggested default |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | What is the unit of truth: RSVP counts or host-confirmed "on this week" status? **Adopted 2026-09-06.** | Research found attendees mostly want to know whether this week's meet is happening, where to park, and what will show. RSVPs are noisy for free, recurring, walk-up events. | Both, as specified: `events.last_confirmed_at` with `POST /events/:id/confirm`, "Last confirmed" on cards and detail, RSVP as soft interest, check-in and photos as crowd signals in Phase 4 (`docs/specs/events-and-occurrences.md`, `event-detail-and-rsvp.md`). |
| 5 | Who owns seeded meets? **Adopted 2026-09-06.** | About 70 recurring SoCal meets exist as seed data, but hosts have not claimed them. Unclaimed events need someone who can edit and cancel, and stale seeds erode trust fast (directories already have wrong venues and times). | The app account (`curb`) is host of record and owner of seeded clubs. Cards show "Unclaimed" with a claim path. `stale` at 30 days and `dormant_at` at 90 days on `COALESCE(last_confirmed_at, published_at)` for unclaimed events; dormant events leave lists but keep their page (`docs/specs/events-and-occurrences.md` R-25 to R-28). |
| 6 | How much of the seed list is verified against the organizer's Instagram before launch? | Directory data disagrees with organizers on venue, time, and status. | Verify every seeded meet by hand in Phase 1 (the plan budgets this). Store a source link and a verification date on each record. |
| 7 | Irregular cadences ("dates announced on IG", seasonal, first Fridays) | RRULE handles regular patterns. Announced-only meets need a way to exist without a false schedule. | Support cadence types: weekly rule, monthly rule, announced (no occurrences until the host posts), seasonal (rule with date bounds). |
| 8 | Launch region boundary. **Updated 2026-09-05.** | Density beats breadth for a cold-start marketplace. The rebrand moves the beachhead to coastal Orange County, where the research inventory is thinnest (about six coastal meets captured), while the Inland Empire has about 20 captured meets and is where the builder can attend every weekend. | Two rings. Ring one: Newport Beach, Corona del Mar, Laguna Beach, Dana Point, San Clemente (25 verified meets by TestFlight, 40 by launch, seeded largely by hand). Ring two: the Inland Empire (25 by TestFlight, 45 by launch). LA and San Diego added once hosts in both rings are active. |
| 9 | Venues as a first-class object at MVP? | Coffee shop and dealership partnerships are in the go-to-market, but venue pages are marked Later. | Keep the venues table in the MVP data model (it is already there for geo), defer venue profile pages. |
| 10 | Web scope at launch. **Resolved 2026-09-06: read-only.** | Read-only SEO surface with RSVP redirecting to the app, or full sign-in and RSVP on web? Clubs and sponsors added a management question: clubs are managed on the app and web, sponsors on the web only, which would need signed-in web. | Read-only plus share and directions at launch, including club, sponsor, and spot pages. Clubs and sponsors are managed through the admin-only Rails UI at launch (`docs/specs/admin.md`); self-service web management, web sign-in, and RSVP are Phase 7. |
| 11 | Contested lots, street takeovers, and liability. **Adopted 2026-09-06, legal review still pending.** | Some SoCal meets attract takeovers, and organizers fear shutdowns (Crystal Cove, Irvine, San Clemente permit fight). Listing a meet at a lot where the property owner objects creates exposure. Spots (photo locations) add the same exposure for private property. | Terms require hosts to have venue permission. `claim_requests.venue_permission_confirmed` is required, `events.venue_permission_confirmed_at` is set on approval, spots carry an access kind and notes, and `unauthorized_location` is a report reason for events and spots (`docs/specs/moderation-and-safety.md`, `spots.md`). Get a one-hour legal review before public launch. |
| 12 | Moderation SLA and backup | Solo builder is the moderator of record. What happens during vacation or a viral week? | Report queue with auto-hide after N reports, 24 hour SLA target during beta, a trusted host as backup moderator. |

## Import-from-link

| # | Question | Why it matters | Suggested default |
|---|---|---|---|
| 13 | Evite: what is acceptable? | Evite's terms (Sections 7, 8, 11) prohibit robots, scraping, and data mining, robots.txt disallows /event, and there is no API. Server-side fetching of Evite pages is off the table. | Primary path is user-pasted text or a share-sheet share from Evite into the app, then LLM extraction into a draft. At most a single device-side OG preview fetch initiated by the user. Confirm with a lawyer before shipping even that. |
| 14 | Instagram and Facebook. **Resolved 2026-09-06 for photos (ADR 0011); import stays as below.** | Meta bans automated collection for both, logged in or out. oEmbed is display-only. Facebook is the most common host channel. Personal Instagram accounts have had no API access since the Basic Display API sunset in September 2025; only Business and Creator accounts can grant media access, and that needs Meta App Review. | Import: share-sheet share or paste of the caption text, no fetch, no image. Photos: an Instagram post shared from the share sheet becomes a post of kind `instagram` rendered through oEmbed and never stored (`docs/specs/photos-and-posts.md`). Instagram Login for professional accounts is Phase 7. Facebook: paste text only. |
| 15 | Imported cover images | Copying an Evite or Instagram image is a rights problem even if the host pasted the link. | Imported events use a branded placeholder (flat, marine-layer band, no photo) until the host uploads a photo or explicitly confirms rights. |
| 16 | LLM extraction vendor, cost, and privacy | Pasted invite text may contain names, addresses, and phone numbers. | Use a structured-output model call with PII minimization, do not log raw payloads beyond 30 days, budget about $10 to $30 per month at beta volume. Vendor choice is open. |
| 17 | Eventbrite and Meetup | Eventbrite has an official API but requires attribution and forbids storing past events. Meetup's API is behind a Pro subscription. | Eventbrite adapter by event ID in Phase 3. Meetup via JSON-LD on the public page as best effort. |

## Brand and design

The rename and the direction (quiet coastal classic, flat palette, editorial serif plus grotesk, three themes) are decided; see ADR 0009 and `brand/brand-guide.md`. These are the decisions still open inside that direction.

| # | Question | Why it matters | Suggested default |
|---|---|---|---|
| 26 | Which theme ships as the default | The brief names Marine Layer as default, but none of the three has been seen on a device under Liquid Glass, where the system pulls color from content beneath the glass. The default sets the App Store screenshots, the OG cards, and the imported-event placeholder. | Marine Layer, in its light variant, following the system appearance for dark. Build all three into `packages/design-tokens` from the start, ship a theme picker in Settings, and confirm the default after the Phase 0 design QA pass on device. |
| 27 | Which wordmark and monogram option | Two directions are drafted: (a) a custom lowercase "curb" wordmark with one distinctive letterform detail plus a CURB SOCIAL CLUB small-caps lockup, and (b) a curb-profile monogram (step or chamfer cross-section forming a C). The app icon, favicon, OG card, and Story cards all need one answer. | Use both, with the wordmark leading: the wordmark in the app header, web, and marketing; the monogram as the app icon, favicon, and map avatar. Pick the specific letterform detail and the chamfer-versus-step variant after reviewing the icon at 16 px and 1024 px on a device, and confirm no car or cup silhouette reads into either mark. |
| 28 | Where the serif appears: tab bar labels or content only | Instrument Serif in tab labels would make the whole app read editorial, but native Liquid Glass tabs render their own SF Pro labels, and a custom-font tab bar means a custom tab bar. Headlines, event titles, and the wordmark are the agreed serif surfaces. | Content only. Tab bar labels, sheets, and system chrome stay SF Pro; the serif appears in screen headlines, event titles, section headers, and the wordmark. Revisit only if the native tab bar is replaced for another reason. |
| 29 | Audience tension: coastal classic brand versus an open product | The brand is written for the Newport Beach classic Porsche owner; the product principle is "easy and useful, not exclusive." A first-timer in a daily driver, or an Inland Empire host, must not read the app as "not for me," and the beachhead persona must not read it as generic. | The brand is a tone, not a velvet rope. No marque filters, no curated tier, no invite codes, identical cards for every meet. Copy passes a two-way test (tasteful to the beachhead persona, welcoming to a first-timer). Onboarding copy is tested with two or three non-enthusiasts before beta. If the two readings ever conflict, the open product wins and the brand adjusts. |
| 30 | Pin taxonomy and confidence chip labels | "now, today, upcoming, recurring, past" and "Sure, Check, Guess" drive filters and copy. The new voice (calm, specific, dry) may want plainer labels. | Settle early; they are cheap to change now and expensive later. Keep the pin states. Consider "Confirmed, Check, Guess" for confidence to match the "Confirmed by host" language in item 4. |
| 31 | Figma plan. **Resolved 2026-09-05: upgraded to Professional.** | Starter allowed 20 MCP calls per month, one mode per variable collection, and three pages per file, which blocked the three-theme variable modes and the scripted screens. | Rebuild the file for the new brand: one variable collection with six modes (three themes by light and dark), foundations, components, and screens. Retire the old amber file rather than converting it. |

## Things the docs assume that Amir has not confirmed

The stack (Rails 8 API, React, Expo), monorepo tooling (pnpm plus Turborepo), hosting (Render, Vercel, R2), auth (Sign in with Apple and Google, anonymous browse), private GitHub repo, solo nights-and-weekends cadence, the rename to Curb Social Club, the brand direction, the three themes, the coastal Orange County beachhead with the Inland Empire as the second ring, and the 2026-09-06 scope decisions (polymorphic hosts, clubs and sponsors read-only at launch with admin-only management, read-only web, Instagram by share sheet and oEmbed, spots as a first-class object) were all confirmed. Judgment calls made while writing the specs that Amir has not explicitly confirmed: hand-written admin views instead of an admin gem, four tabs with notifications behind a bell, the 32 km default radius, the 30 and 90 day decay clock, the 150 m and 25 m spot thresholds, and the June launch target. The domain (`curbsocial.club`), bundle id (`club.curbsocial.app`), and social handles are placeholders. Everything else in the docs marked Proposed, Suggested, or Placeholder is a judgment call by the planning workstreams and is open to change.
