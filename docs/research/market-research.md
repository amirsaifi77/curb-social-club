# Cars and Coffee: Market Research

Workstream: market research. Date: 2026-09-05. Scope: how car meets are discovered today, competing apps and directories, trademark exposure of the name, a SoCal / Inland Empire seed inventory, import feasibility for Evite and other sources, and audience behavior.

Method note: roughly 50 web searches and page fetches. Several primary sources (Facebook groups, Instagram profiles, Yelp, evite.com invitation pages, Meta's terms page) block automated fetching, so those claims lean on secondary reporting and are flagged where relevant. Everything else links to the page it came from.

## 1. How meets are organized and discovered today

The short version: there is no system of record. A recurring meet lives on an Instagram account and a Facebook page, gets mirrored (often late or wrong) onto a handful of volunteer-run directory sites, and spreads by word of mouth in group chats and forums. One-off meets are announced by flyer image on Instagram and Facebook, and increasingly by Eventbrite or Partiful links dropped into chats.

### Instagram (primary channel for recurring meets)

Nearly every SoCal meet has an Instagram handle and uses it as the canonical source for "are we on this week." Examples: South OC Cars and Coffee at [@southoccarsandcoffee](https://www.instagram.com/southoccarsandcoffee/), Supercar Sunday at [@supercarsunday_official](https://supercarsunday.com/pages/about-us), Cars & Coffee Chino Hills at [@carsandcoffeechinohills](https://www.instagram.com/carsandcoffeechinohills/), Redlands at [@redlandscarsandcoffee](https://www.instagram.com/redlandscarsandcoffee/) and [@dtrcarsandcoffee](https://www.instagram.com/dtrcarsandcoffee/), Inland Empire Cars and Coffee at [@inlandempirecarsandcofffee](https://www.instagram.com/inlandempirecarsandcofffee/) (note the triple f), Cars and Coffee Aliso Viejo at [@carsandcoffee_alisoviejo](https://www.instagram.com/carsandcoffee_alisoviejo/), and regional aggregator accounts like [@ie_cars](https://www.instagram.com/ie_cars/) and [@carmeetscalifornia](https://www.instagram.com/carmeetscalifornia/).

Hashtag volume is large: #carsandcoffee has roughly 4.5 million posts with about 8.7K new uses per week, and city variants such as #carsandcoffeeirvine (17.6K) and #carsandcoffeedallas (45K) show how localized the tag culture is ([best-hashtags.com](https://best-hashtags.com/hashtag/carsandcoffee/)). The LA guide on Cars & Coffee Directory tells readers to "follow local car Instagram accounts for meetup details" for Malibu and to monitor @carsandcoffeela for "schedule changes and pop-up events" ([Cars & Coffee Directory LA guide](https://www.carsandcoffeedirectory.com/blog/cars-and-coffee-los-angeles-2026-guide)). The organizer of Cars, Coffee and Bagels in Rancho Cucamonga lists Instagram ([@carsil0g](https://www.idrivesocal.com/events/cars-coffee-and-bagels)) as the sole contact, and Inland Empire Cars and Coffee at Tom's Farms routes vehicle registration through [@wicked_syndicate](https://www.idrivesocal.com/events/inland-empire-cars-and-coffee). The pattern for one-offs is a flyer image in a post, with the date, time and address only in the image or caption.

### Facebook pages, groups, and Events

Facebook remains the second home for recurring meets, especially older-skewing classic and cruise-night crowds. Cars And Coffee Corona points to [facebook.com/carscoffeecorona](https://www.idrivesocal.com/events/cars-and-coffee-corona), Chino Hills to [facebook.com/carsandcoffeechinohills](https://www.idrivesocal.com/events/cars-and-coffee-chino-hills), CBad Cars and Coffee to [facebook.com/cbadcars](https://www.idrivesocal.com/events/cbad-cars-and-coffee), Riverside Cars & Coffee to [facebook.com/RiversideCarsandCoffee](https://www.facebook.com/RiversideCarsandCoffee/), and Redlands Cars and Coffee to a Facebook group ([iDriveSoCal](https://www.idrivesocal.com/events/redlands-cars-and-coffee)). Regional groups act as the question-and-answer layer: [Inland Empire Car Meets And Cruises](https://www.facebook.com/groups/532314626842929/), [So Cal Cruise Nights and Car Shows IE, OC, LA](https://www.facebook.com/groups/1604083679860489/), [So Cal Car Shows](https://www.facebook.com/groups/1577942295807497/), [Inland Empire Cars & Coffee Saturday](https://www.facebook.com/IECarsNCoffeeSaturday/), and the page [SO CAL CAR MEETS/SHOWS](https://www.facebook.com/SocalCarMeetsShows). A representative group post is literally titled "Are there any car shows in the Inland Empire this weekend" ([Facebook group post](https://www.facebook.com/groups/3392886350741549/posts/24545711538365723/)), which is the discovery problem in one sentence. Member counts could not be fetched because Facebook blocks automated access.

### Meetup, Eventbrite, Partiful, Evite, Apple Invites

Meetup has a "cars & coffee" topic with groups in LA ([Meetup topic page](https://www.meetup.com/topics/cars-coffee/us/ca/los_angeles/)) and dedicated groups like [San Mateo Cars & Coffee](https://www.meetup.com/san-mateo-cars-coffee/), but it is a minor channel in SoCal. Eventbrite carries searchable "cars and coffee" pages for [Rancho Cucamonga](https://www.eventbrite.com/d/ca--rancho-cucamonga/cars-and-coffee/), [Murrieta](https://www.eventbrite.com/d/ca--murrieta/cars-and-coffee/), and [Chino Hills](https://www.eventbrite.com/d/ca--chino-hills/cars-and-coffee/), and the LA directory tells readers to "check Eventbrite for dates" for a DTLA meet ([LA guide](https://www.carsandcoffeedirectory.com/blog/cars-and-coffee-los-angeles-2026-guide)). Evite invitation pages for cars and coffee events exist in the wild (an indexed example: [evite.com/event/00B4AFQWO5SX24F6UEPQJXW46HD5KA](https://www.evite.com/event/00B4AFQWO5SX24F6UEPQJXW46HD5KA)), typically for dealership or club-hosted meets such as the Herb Chambers dealership series covered by [Patch](https://patch.com/massachusetts/sharon/ev--herb-chambers-lexus-hosts-cars-and-coffee). Partiful is a text-link invitation product with "millions" of monthly active users and 95 percent of downloads on iOS ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/11/18/partiful-is-googles-best-app-of-2024/)); it skews to younger hosts and private parties, not recurring public meets, but it is the format group-chat organizers now reach for.

### Hosts: dealerships, coffee shops, donut shops, breweries, churches, malls

Venues are the other constant. In the iDriveSoCal inventory the hosts are Donut Star (Chino Hills, Menifee), Dickey's BBQ (Rancho Cucamonga), Cassidy's Corner Cafe (Rancho Cucamonga), Escape Craft Brewery (Redlands), Harvest Christian Fellowship (Riverside), J & M Speed Center (Riverside), Tom's Farms (Temescal Valley), Archibald's Drive-Thru (Menifee), the Outlets at San Clemente, the Shops at Dos Lagos (Corona), Segerstrom Shelby Event Center (Irvine), and Pierce College (Woodland Hills) ([iDriveSoCal Saturday](https://www.idrivesocal.com/events/saturday), [Sunday](https://www.idrivesocal.com/events/sunday)). Venue-hosted meets often publicize through the venue's own Instagram (Dickey's uses [@dickeysbbqrancho](https://www.idrivesocal.com/events/dickey-s-rancho-cucamonga-car-meet)) or mall event calendars ([Outlets at San Clemente event page](https://www.outletsatsanclemente.com/event/18747-cars-and-coffee)). Municipalities also host: the City of Fontana runs a First Fridays car show at the Civic Center, March through September ([iDriveSoCal](https://www.idrivesocal.com/events/fontana-car-show), [fontana.org](https://www.fontana.org/1106/Fontana-Car-Shows)).

### Clubs, forums, Discord, word of mouth

Club sites and forums coordinate attendance: the SoCal MG Club posts Supercar Sunday as a club outing ([socalmgclub.org](https://www.socalmgclub.org/event-details-registration/pierce-college-super-car-sunday-2024-02-04-07-00)), the Porsche 718 Forum has a "SoCal Cars & Coffee Meetup?" thread ([718forum](https://www.718forum.com/threads/socal-cars-coffee-meetup.22149/)), and CorvetteForum has a multi-page Aliso Viejo thread ([corvetteforum](https://www.corvetteforum.com/forums/southern-california/3691115-car-and-coffee-aliso-viejo-2.html)). Discord has general car servers (the [Car Community](https://discord.com/invite/cars) server, [Cali Cars](https://discord.me/calicars), and a [car-meets tag](https://discord.me/servers/tag/car-meets)) but nothing that functions as a SoCal meet calendar. Organizers themselves describe growth as word of mouth: South OC's founders say they rely on community word-of-mouth and brand partnerships ([San Clemente Times](https://www.picketfencemedia.com/sanclementetimes/eye-on-sc/south-oc-cars-coffee-celebrates-9-years-of-community-growth/article_3bcdefbe-6afb-40a5-8c2c-f9309822cd4b.html)), and the original Irvine meet drew about 1,000 cars per Saturday "all without any advertising" ([Capital One](https://www.capitalone.com/cars/learn/finding-the-right-car/the-origin-story-of-cars-and-coffee/3675)). CBad in Carlsbad grew "thanks in part to all the posts done by RallyWays and all the other photographers that attend every weekend" ([RallyWays](https://rallyways.com/225/saturday-mornings-cars-and-coffee-carlsbad-california/)), which is a useful signal that photo output is a discovery engine.

## 2. Competing and adjacent platforms

Nobody owns this category. The dedicated apps are small indie projects with tens of ratings, the directories are volunteer-run websites with no social layer, and the big general platforms (Instagram, Facebook, Eventbrite, Meetup) have the audience but no car-specific structure. DriveTribe, the one well-funded attempt at a car social network, closed in January 2022.

### What this says about the opening

Every dedicated app is solo-developer scale with fewer than 50 ratings, and each one starts from an empty map. The directories have data but no product. Nobody has built the obvious bridge: import from Instagram, Facebook, Evite, Eventbrite where organizers already post, then layer map, recurrence, RSVP and photos on top. The brief's "meet people where they're at" principle is exactly the gap. The one thing to watch is carsandcoffee.com, which holds the exact-match domain and is collecting emails for a national aggregator ([carsandcoffee.com](https://carsandcoffee.com/)).

PORTLAND CARS & COFFEE (Reg 4980087) survived a cancellation attempt but sits on the Supplemental Register ([Justia](https://trademarks.justia.com/866/62/portland-cars-86662353.html)), which is where descriptive terms go. Indianapolis Cars and Coffee Inc. filed INDIANAPOLIS CARS & COFFEE in 2024 ([TrademarkElite](https://www.trademarkelite.com/trademark/trademark-owner/Cars%20and%20Coffee,%20Inc./1)).

Reading across these records: the USPTO has repeatedly refused or required disclaimers on "cars and coffee" for event and club services (classes 035 and 041), which is consistent with the Office treating the phrase as descriptive or generic for the events themselves. No one holds a live Principal Register registration for CARS AND COFFEE covering events or software. The refusal grounds are not visible in the summaries fetched; pull the office actions from TSDR to confirm (likely Section 2(e)(1) descriptiveness).

### History and enforcement

The event started at Crystal Cove in 2003, was shut down by the Irvine Company on October 14, 2006 over noise complaints, and moved to a Mazda and Ford lot in Irvine where John Clinard (Ford PR) and Barry Meguiar helped name it Cars and Coffee ([DrivingLine](https://www.drivingline.com/articles/cars-coffee-the-real-beginnings-at-crystal-cove/), [Capital One](https://www.capitalone.com/cars/learn/finding-the-right-car/the-origin-story-of-cars-and-coffee/3675)). It ran 410 consecutive Saturdays and closed in December 2014 over capacity and neighbor complaints ([Hagerty](https://www.hagerty.com/media/archived/californias-granddaddy-cars-and-coffee-reunion/), [DrivingLine](https://www.drivingline.com/articles/gone-but-never-forgotten-irvine-cars-coffee/)). The format has since spread to more than 200 US cities ([Capital One](https://www.capitalone.com/cars/learn/finding-the-right-car/the-origin-story-of-cars-and-coffee/3675)). No reporting of cease-and-desist enforcement against local organizers using the phrase was found in this research; hundreds of independent "X Cars and Coffee" events operate openly, and the Instagram account @carsandcoffee styles itself "Cars & Coffee ™" ([Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/carsandcoffee/)), a TM claim rather than a registration symbol.

### Naming risk assessment and options (for decision, not decided here)

Risk is concentrated, not broad. Using "Cars and Coffee" as an app name for event discovery software (class 009 or 042) is unlikely to be blocked by the clothing registration, and the failed 035/041 filings suggest the Office would also refuse the app's own registration as descriptive. The practical exposures are: (a) inability to register or defend the name, so anyone can clone it; (b) App Store and domain confusion with carsandcoffee.com, which is pre-launching a national aggregator under "Cars & Coffee LLC" ([carsandcoffee.com](https://carsandcoffee.com/)); (c) a merchandise line would collide with Reg 4941522; (d) a nuisance letter from Cars and Coffee, Inc. or the Italian company even if weak. None of this is legal advice; a trademark attorney should run a clearance search on TSDR and the state registers before launch.

Options to weigh:

1. Use "Cars and Coffee" as the descriptive category and a distinctive brand as the name (for example "Meetline: cars and coffee near you"). Registrable, clear of the clothing mark, keeps SEO on the generic phrase.
2. A coined or suggestive name that evokes the ritual without the phrase (morning, lot, idle, parked, warmup, pour, torque). Strongest legal position, most marketing work.
3. Keep "Cars and Coffee" as the working title and file an intent-to-use application in classes 009 and 042 early to test the Office's reaction; budget for a refusal and a fallback.
4. Use "Cars & Coffee" with a geographic or modifier prefix (the Portland, Lehigh Valley, Indianapolis pattern), which the Office tolerates with a disclaimer but which limits national expansion.

## 5. Import feasibility by source

The signature feature is paste-a-link to scaffold a draft event. The question for each source is what a public URL exposes, what the terms allow, and how brittle the parse is. Ratings: Green (official API or explicit permission), Yellow (technically feasible, terms gray or fragile), Red (terms prohibit or access is gated).

### Evite: Yellow-Red for automated fetch, Green for user-pasted text

What is exposed: Evite offers two products, Invitations and Event Pages. Both are shared by link (email, text, or copied "shareable link"), and guests RSVP directly on the page ([Evite blog](https://www.evite.com/blog/planning-budget/what-are-evite-event-pages/), [Evite support: shareable link](https://support.evite.com/products/invitations/manage-and-edit-guest-list/shareable-link-for-invitation)). Invitation URLs take the form `evite.com/event/<opaque id>` (indexed example: [evite.com/event/00B4AFQWO5SX24F6UEPQJXW46HD5KA](https://www.evite.com/event/00B4AFQWO5SX24F6UEPQJXW46HD5KA)). Hosts can hide the guest list but Evite's docs do not describe hiding the date, time, or location from link holders ([Evite support: hide guest list](https://support.evite.com/products/invitations/manage-and-edit-guest-list/hide-guest-list)); a support forum thread reports some recipients being prompted to log in to view ([JustAnswer](https://www.justanswer.com/software/qzd24-recipients-evite-sent-yesterday-tell.html)), so assume login gating is possible on some invite types. The invitation page itself could not be fetched here because Evite's robots.txt disallows `/event` for all user agents except an allow-list ([evite.com/robots.txt](https://www.evite.com/robots.txt)), so the exact HTML, Open Graph and JSON payload structure remains unverified. Expect a client-rendered React app that hydrates from an internal JSON endpoint, plus og:title and og:image for link previews in iMessage (Evite must serve those for previews to work), but confirm with a real invite before designing the parser.

Terms: Evite's Terms of Service (updated May 20, 2026) prohibit using "any robot, spider, site search/retrieval application or other manual or automatic device to retrieve, index, 'scrape,' 'data mine' or in any way gather Content or Materials from the Service" (Section 11), exclude "any use of data mining, robots, or similar data gathering and extraction tools" from the license (Section 7), and bar reproducing or creating derivative works from Content without written authorization (Section 8) ([Evite mobile terms](https://www.evite.com/mobile/terms/)). There is no public API or developer program ([Evite mobile terms](https://www.evite.com/mobile/terms/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evite)).

Recommendation: do not run a server-side fetcher against evite.com. Two compliant paths. (1) Client-side: the invited user opens the Evite link in an in-app WebView or SFSafariViewController, then taps "Share" to send the page text to the app, or copies the page and pastes it; the app runs LLM extraction over the pasted text. Nothing automated touches Evite. (2) Link preview only: fetch og:title, og:description, og:image the way iMessage does (a single fetch on user action, not crawling) and let the user fill in the rest. Path 2 still technically conflicts with Section 11's wording, so route it through the user's device rather than the server, and ask counsel whether a single user-initiated preview fetch is acceptable. Reliability of either path is high because the LLM extraction does not depend on Evite's markup.

### Partiful: Yellow

What is exposed: public Partiful events show title, description, date and time, location, host, cover image and often the guest list to anyone with the link; links use high-entropy IDs, and hosts can restrict visibility, hide location, or require approval ([Security Scientist](https://www.securityscientist.net/blog/12-questions-and-answers-about-partiful-event-privacy/)). Partiful's robots.txt explicitly allows social preview bots including facebookexternalhit, Twitterbot, Slackbot, Discordbot, and WhatsApp ([partiful.com/robots.txt](https://partiful.com/robots.txt)), so Open Graph tags are reliably served. Third-party scrapers extract title, date, location, guest count, host, cover image and URL without login ([Apify Partiful scraper](https://apify.com/heliostech/partiful-event-scraper/api)); an unofficial client uses reverse-engineered Firebase endpoints with manually copied bearer tokens that expire ([cerebralvalley/partiful-api](https://github.com/cerebralvalley/partiful-api)).

Terms: users may not "engage in or use any data mining, robots, scraping, or similar data gathering or extraction methods," may not "copy, frame, scrape ... or create derivative works," and must not circumvent blocks; no API is offered ([Partiful terms](https://partiful.com/terms)).

Recommendation: Open Graph fetch on user action (the same thing WhatsApp does when a link is pasted) is the defensible line, giving title, description and image; date and address usually appear in og:description but are not guaranteed. Fall back to user-pasted text plus LLM extraction. Do not use the reverse-engineered API. Reliability: medium (OG format can change without notice).

### Eventbrite: Green

Official API v3 with OAuth 2.0; retrieve a single event with `GET /v3/events/{id}/` and expand venue, organizer and logo; deprecated public event search was turned off February 20, 2020, but retrieval by ID, by venue and by organization still works ([Eventbrite docs](https://www.eventbrite.com/platform/docs/events), [Automattic issue](https://github.com/Automattic/eventbrite-api/issues/83), [I'd Rather Be Writing](https://idratherbewriting.com/learnapidoc/docapis_eventbrite_example.html)). Rate limit 1,000 calls per hour per token and 48,000 per day ([Rollout guide](https://rollout.com/integration-guides/eventbrite/api-essentials)). API terms require showing the event title with a crawlable, non-nofollow link back to the Eventbrite page, allow storing content for future events but not past ones without user permission, prohibit building a competing product, and require clear "not owned by Eventbrite" branding ([Eventbrite API Terms of Use](https://www.eventbrite.com/help/en-us/articles/833731/eventbrite-api-terms-of-use/)). Event pages also expose schema.org Event JSON-LD, since Eventbrite is Google's showcase case study for event structured data ([Google case study](https://developers.google.com/search/case-studies/eventbrite-case-study)).

Recommendation: parse the event ID from the URL, call the API with an app token, map name, description, start, end, venue address and lat/long, logo. Keep the source link and purge past-event copies per the terms. Reliability: high. Watch the "competing product" clause; a discovery app that links out to Eventbrite ticketing is the intended use, but a ticketing feature later would breach it.

### Meetup: Yellow

The REST API is gone; the GraphQL API at `api.meetup.com/gql-ext` uses Bearer tokens via OAuth2, returns title, description, dateTime, eventUrl, venue, eventHosts, featuredEventPhoto and rsvps, and enforces 500 points per 60 seconds ([Meetup GraphQL guide](https://www.meetup.com/graphql/guide/)). Access requires an active Meetup Pro subscription to apply, and Pro "does not guarantee approval"; the REST API was shut off in February 2025 ([Meetup help](https://help.meetup.com/hc/en-us/articles/41453576628749-How-can-I-get-access-to-Meetup-s-API)). Public event pages still ship JSON-LD and `__NEXT_DATA__` hydration payloads containing title, ISO start, venue and counts ([logiover](https://logiover.com/guides/meetup-api-alternative-event-group-data/)).

Recommendation: given Meetup's low share of SoCal car meets, defer the API. If needed, JSON-LD parse of the public page on user action is the pragmatic route; it is standard structured data intended for consumption, but confirm against Meetup's terms before shipping. Reliability: medium.

### Facebook Events: Red for automated; Yellow for organizer-authorized

Meta's terms prohibit accessing or collecting data "using automated means (without our prior permission)," defined to include scrapers, bots, spiders and crawlers, and since January 1, 2025 the ban applies "regardless of whether such automated access or collection is undertaken while logged-in" ([facebookscraperapi.com analysis](https://facebookscraperapi.com/blog/is-scraping-facebook-legal)); Meta rewrote the terms after a court found the pre-2025 wording did not cover logged-off scraping in Meta v. Bright Data ([same source](https://facebookscraperapi.com/blog/is-scraping-facebook-legal)). Graph API access to page content requires Page Public Content Access, App Review, business verification, and during development only works on pages the developer admins; group content is not available at all ([SocialCrawl 2026 overview](https://www.socialcrawl.dev/blog/facebook-data-api-2026)). Event pages are typically behind a login wall, which is exactly the "gates down" condition that distinguishes Facebook from the hiQ v. LinkedIn public-data holding ([SocialCrawl](https://www.socialcrawl.dev/blog/facebook-data-api-2026)).

Recommendation: no server-side fetching. Support Facebook via (a) user pastes the event text or shares a screenshot, then LLM or OCR extraction, and (b) later, an organizer-side "connect your Page" OAuth flow using the Pages API after App Review, which lets a host import their own events legitimately. Reliability of the paste path: high; of the API path: slow to obtain.

### Instagram: Yellow

The oEmbed endpoint returns embed HTML for public posts and Reels with an app token, 1,000 requests per hour, but Meta states it is "only meant to be used for embedding" and using the metadata "for any purpose other than providing a front-end view" is prohibited; private, age-restricted and embed-disabled accounts are unsupported ([Meta oEmbed docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/oembed/)). Captions are available through the Graph API media object, but reading other accounts requires Business Discovery (target must be a Professional account) or hashtag search (30 hashtags per 7 days, no username), and any third-party use needs full App Review with business verification ([Instagram API reference gist](https://gist.github.com/jameschapman2c/65eff9f54a2d350b17a6ce5127b9fe42)). The Basic Display API was sunset in September 2025 ([same gist](https://gist.github.com/jameschapman2c/65eff9f54a2d350b17a6ce5127b9fe42)).

Recommendation: for the MVP, treat an Instagram link as a flyer: the user shares the post to the app (iOS share sheet delivers the URL; the user can also long-press to copy the caption or screenshot the flyer), then run OCR on the image and LLM extraction on caption text. Store the source URL and render it with the official oEmbed embed, which is the sanctioned use. Do not scrape instagram.com. Reliability: medium, since flyer typography defeats OCR sometimes; keep the draft editable.

### Flyer image (OCR plus LLM): Green

No third-party terms involved. Apple's Vision framework handles on-device text recognition; feed the recognized lines to an LLM prompt that returns title, date, time window, address, host and rules. TheCarShowsApp already accepts flyer upload as its primary submission path ([TheCarShowsApp](https://www.thecarshowsapp.com/car-shows-app)), which validates the pattern. Reliability: medium to high with human confirmation.

| Partiful | Title, description, date, location, cover, guest count | Prohibits scraping; no API; allows preview bots | Yellow | OG preview on user action; paste fallback |
| Eventbrite | Everything via API and JSON-LD | Official API with attribution and storage rules | Green | API by event ID |
| Meetup | JSON-LD and Next.js payload on public pages; GraphQL with Pro | Pro subscription plus approval for API | Yellow | Defer; JSON-LD parse if needed |
| Facebook Events | Login-walled | Explicit ban on automated collection, logged in or out | Red | Paste or screenshot; organizer OAuth later |
| Instagram | oEmbed embed only; captions via reviewed Graph API | oEmbed limited to embedding | Yellow | Share-sheet plus OCR and caption LLM; official embed for display |
| Flyer image | N/A | N/A | Green | On-device OCR plus LLM |
| Pasted text | N/A | N/A | Green | LLM extraction; the universal fallback |

The architectural implication: the importer interface should be `text plus optional image plus optional structured API payload` in, `draft event` out. Eventbrite is the only source that deserves a true API adapter at launch; everything else converges on the LLM-over-text-and-image path with a source URL stored for link-out.

## 6. Audience and behavior

### Who and when

Meets are weekend mornings, most starting between 7 and 9 AM and running two to three hours ([Cars & Coffee Directory etiquette](https://www.carsandcoffeedirectory.com/blog/cars-and-coffee-etiquette-unwritten-rules), [Garage app guide](https://garageapp.com/blog/shows-events/cars-and-coffee-events-guide/)); the SoCal inventory above confirms the 7 to 10 AM cluster, with a minority of afternoon and First Friday evening events. Attendance ranges from a couple dozen cars at a donut shop to 500 to 600 at South OC ([southoccarsandcoffee.com](https://www.southoccarsandcoffee.com/)) and the historical 1,000-car Irvine peak ([Capital One](https://www.capitalone.com/cars/learn/finding-the-right-car/the-origin-story-of-cars-and-coffee/3675)). The crowd mixes owners displaying cars with spectators and families; parents are told to keep kids within arm's reach and ask before letting them sit in cars ([Autoblog](https://www.autoblog.com/features/these-are-the-dos-and-donts-of-attending-your-first-cars-and-coffee), [Chariotz](https://www.chariotz.com/blog/the-unwritten-rules-of-cars-and-coffee-what-newcomers-should-know/)). South OC times its meet to outlet-store opening so non-enthusiasts wander in ([iDriveSoCal](https://www.idrivesocal.com/events/south-oc-cars-and-coffee)), and Redlands explicitly welcomes "kids, families and pets" ([iDriveSoCal](https://www.idrivesocal.com/events/redlands-cars-and-coffee)).

### What people care about

Arrival timing and parking dominate first-timer questions. Regulars arrive 30 or more minutes before the listed start, the best spots fill within 30 minutes, display parking differs from spectator parking, and owners often leave before the official end ([Chariotz](https://www.chariotz.com/blog/the-unwritten-rules-of-cars-and-coffee-what-newcomers-should-know/), [Cars & Coffee Directory etiquette](https://www.carsandcoffeedirectory.com/blog/cars-and-coffee-etiquette-unwritten-rules), [Autoblog](https://www.autoblog.com/features/these-are-the-dos-and-donts-of-attending-your-first-cars-and-coffee)). Rules and police matter because meets get shut down: Crystal Cove and Irvine both ended over neighbor complaints ([DrivingLine](https://www.drivingline.com/articles/cars-coffee-the-real-beginnings-at-crystal-cove/), [DrivingLine](https://www.drivingline.com/articles/gone-but-never-forgotten-irvine-cars-coffee/)); Houston Coffee & Cars permanently banned Mustangs, Camaros, Chargers and Challengers in March 2024 after burnouts near crowds with children and moved toward invitation-only ([The Autopian](https://www.theautopian.com/why-this-cars-and-coffee-just-permanently-banned-all-modern-muscle-cars-like-camaros-challengers-and-mustangs/comment-page-1/)); San Clemente's council reviewed South OC's permit in January 2026 over noise, speeding and a proposed $1,350 per week deputy cost before about 50 public speakers persuaded the city to keep it going with existing patrols ([Voice of OC](https://voiceofoc.org/2026/01/the-show-goes-on-for-south-oc-cars-and-coffee/), [ABC7](https://abc7.com/post/south-oc-cars-coffee-event-san-clemente-jeopardy-city-reviews-permit/18418583/)). Organizers publish rules pages (Supercar Sunday's "responsible behavior and safe driving" page, [supercarsunday.com](https://supercarsunday.com/pages/about-us)) and listings carry "no burnouts, no revving, no loud music" language ([Riverside listing](https://www.idrivesocal.com/events/riverside-cars-and-coffee), [Tom's Farms listing](https://www.idrivesocal.com/events/inland-empire-cars-and-coffee)). A Florida meet asked attendees to stop revving for the sake of the manatees ([AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/florida-cars-coffee-event-attendees-180800282.html)). What cars will show is the draw, and coverage emphasizes the unpredictability ("vintage Ferraris, hot rods, tuner cars, rare vehicles, and manufacturer prototypes" at Irvine, [DrivingLine](https://www.drivingline.com/articles/gone-but-never-forgotten-irvine-cars-coffee/)); photographers posting from the meet are what grew CBad ([RallyWays](https://rallyways.com/225/saturday-mornings-cars-and-coffee-carlsbad-california/)). Supporting the host business (buy the coffee, pick up trash) is a repeated norm because venues cancel meets that cost them money ([Cars & Coffee Directory etiquette](https://www.carsandcoffeedirectory.com/blog/cars-and-coffee-etiquette-unwritten-rules)).

### Pain points with discovery today

1. Fragmentation. The 2026 guides tell readers to search directory sites, city Facebook groups, Instagram location tags, Reddit, and sponsor websites, and to verify time, location and focus across them ([Garage app guide](https://garageapp.com/blog/shows-events/cars-and-coffee-events-guide/)); the LA guide says the scene "moves fast" and to watch Instagram for "schedule changes and pop-up events" ([Cars & Coffee Directory LA guide](https://www.carsandcoffeedirectory.com/blog/cars-and-coffee-los-angeles-2026-guide)).
2. Stale and wrong directory data. LACAR still marks Chino Hills "on hold due to Covid" ([LACAR](https://www.lacar.com/car-events-la/cars-and-coffee-chino-hills)) while it runs every Sunday ([iDriveSoCal](https://www.idrivesocal.com/events/cars-and-coffee-chino-hills)); the Cars & Coffee Directory LA guide places Supercar Sunday at Westfield Promenade on the first Sunday monthly ([LA guide](https://www.carsandcoffeedirectory.com/blog/cars-and-coffee-los-angeles-2026-guide)) while the organizer says every Sunday at Pierce College ([supercarsunday.com](https://supercarsunday.com/pages/about-us)); iDriveSoCal shows Cars, Coffee and Bagels with conflicting 8 AM and 10 AM start times on the same page ([iDriveSoCal](https://www.idrivesocal.com/events/cars-coffee-and-bagels)) and "organizer details coming soon" on the Fontana city show ([iDriveSoCal](https://www.idrivesocal.com/events/fontana-car-show)).
3. Question-driven discovery. The Inland Empire Facebook groups run on posts like "Are there any car shows in the Inland Empire this weekend" ([Facebook group post](https://www.facebook.com/groups/3392886350741549/posts/24545711538365723/)), which is a search query with no search engine.
4. Flyers are images. Dates, times and addresses live inside JPEGs on Instagram and PDF flyers on SoCalCarCulture ([SoCalCarCulture](http://www.socalcarculture.com/events.html)), which are not searchable, not mappable, and not calendar-able.
5. Cancellation uncertainty. Whether a weekly meet is on this week (rain, holiday, venue conflict, permit) is broadcast only on the organizer's social account, so attendees must check Instagram the morning of; the guides' advice to "monitor social media for schedule changes" ([LA guide](https://www.carsandcoffeedirectory.com/blog/cars-and-coffee-los-angeles-2026-guide)) is the workaround.
6. Empty apps. Existing car-meet apps have empty maps outside their developer's home region; Strada's reviews specifically note "issues with content population in certain areas" ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/strada-find-car-meets/id1282233083)).

Product implications: a "happening this week?" status per recurring meet (host-confirmed or crowd-confirmed) may be worth more than RSVP counts; arrival-time guidance and parking notes belong on the event detail; rules and family-friendliness should be first-class fields; photo posts from the meet are both content and the growth loop; and the seed inventory must be verified against organizers' Instagram before launch because the directories disagree with each other.
